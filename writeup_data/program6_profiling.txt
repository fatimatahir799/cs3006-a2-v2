Analysis: computeAssignments consistently takes roughly 3.5x longer than
computeCentroids and computeCost combined, across every iteration measured.
This matches the expected asymptotic cost: computeAssignments performs
K x M x N work (checking every point against every centroid), while
computeCentroids and computeCost each perform only M x N work (one pass
per point). With K=3, computeAssignments does roughly 3x the raw work of
the other two functions, consistent with the ~3.5x timing gap observed
(the extra overhead likely comes from computeAssignments' pow()/sqrt()
calls inside dist(), called K times more often than in computeCost).
computeAssignments is therefore the clear hotspot and the target for
parallelization.
