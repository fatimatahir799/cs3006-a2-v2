Program 3 Part 2, mandelbrot_ispc_withtasks, View 1, on Intel i5-7200U (2 cores, AVX2 W=8):

Baseline (launch[2], starter code):
  Speedup: 8.50x

Final (launch[32], 800/32=25 exactly, no truncation issue):
  Speedup: 15.89x

Analysis: My machine's addendum-adjusted ceiling for Program 3 Part 2 is
C x W = 2 physical cores x 8-wide AVX2 = 16x (vs Stanford's 32x figure,
which assumes their 4-core myth machines). 32 tasks was chosen because it
divides height=800 evenly and gives the task scheduler enough small units
of work to spread evenly across both cores, smoothing out the load
imbalance from uneven Mandelbrot iteration counts (same effect as
Program 1's interleaving fix). Achieved 15.89x is essentially at the
theoretical ceiling for this hardware.
