Serial: 288.752 ms
ISPC (SIMD only, AVX2 8-wide): 57.710 ms
Speedup: 5.00x

Theoretical ceiling: W = 8 (AVX2 8-wide single-precision SIMD lanes)

Why observed speedup falls short of the ceiling:
Within an ISPC gang, all lanes execute in lockstep (SIMD). The Mandelbrot
computation has highly variable per-pixel cost -- pixels outside the set
escape after few iterations, pixels inside/near the boundary run up to
maxIterations. Since foreach groups nearby pixels into the same gang, a
gang is only as fast as its slowest lane; fast lanes sit idle (masked off)
until the slowest lane in their gang finishes.
