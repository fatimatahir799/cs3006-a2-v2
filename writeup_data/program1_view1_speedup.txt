Interleaved-row decomposition, View 1, on Intel i5-7200U (2 cores/4 threads):
2 threads: 2.21x
3 threads: 2.97x
4 threads: 3.29x
6 threads: 3.36x
7 threads: 3.33x
8 threads: 3.45x

Conclusion: Speedup plateaus around 3.2-3.4x, matching this machine's
2-core/4-thread ceiling per the addendum's rescaled targets. Basic block
decomposition (equal-sized row strips) causes severe load imbalance since
the Mandelbrot set's compute cost is concentrated near the image center;
interleaved rows spread the expensive computation evenly across threads,
fixing both the imbalance and the crash at thread counts that don't evenly
divide the image height.
